Runway Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Runway handles information when you use the Runway mobile application.

1. Information You Enter

Runway stores the name you choose to enter, budgets, transactions, amounts, categories, notes, bills, savings information, and preferences locally on your device to provide its budgeting features. We do not upload these records to a developer-operated server.

Optional Android SMS suggestions and optional usage analytics handle additional information as explained below. You can use manual budgeting without enabling either feature.

2. Storage, Backups, and Exports

Runway does not operate a cloud database for your financial records. Your device's operating system may back up or transfer the app's local database and preferences according to your device and account settings. The Android SMS suggestion queue is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. Transactions you choose to save, including their notes, become ordinary financial records and may be included in device backups.

If you choose to export or share your records, the data is provided to the destination or app you select. That destination's privacy practices apply to the exported copy.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Runway does not send your financial records, SMS text, or extracted SMS payment details to analytics providers. If you opt in to usage analytics, Google receives the usage and device information described in section 4. Device backups and exports you initiate are described in section 2.

4. Optional Analytics and Advertising

On Android, Runway uses Google Analytics for Firebase only after you agree to share usage analytics. This helps us understand which features are used, where users encounter difficulty, and how to improve the app. Analytics consent is separate from SMS and notification permissions.

Analytics includes events such as opening a feature or successfully saving a transaction, together with standard app and device information, app-instance identifiers, session and engagement information, and approximate location derived from network information. This information is pseudonymous, not a promise of anonymity. Runway does not include your name, financial amounts, balances, transaction dates, categories, notes, SMS sender, SMS text, or extracted payment details in analytics events.

You can change your choice in Settings under Share usage analytics. Turning it off stops future analytics collection and resets local analytics data and the app-instance identifier; it does not automatically delete information already received by Google. Google processes and retains that information under its applicable policies and the analytics service's retention settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

Runway does not show third-party advertisements. Advertising-ID collection and advertising personalization are disabled. This analytics integration is not enabled on iOS.

5. Optional SMS Suggestions and Device Permissions

On Android, you may enable Message suggestions to turn incoming INR payment alerts into suggested transactions. After an in-app explanation and your consent, Runway requests the RECEIVE_SMS permission. While the feature is enabled, it can process new incoming SMS on your device, including while the app is closed, to identify supported debit and credit alerts. It does not read your existing SMS inbox or send SMS messages.

Message text is processed locally and is not stored by Runway or uploaded to us or an analytics provider. Messages that do not match supported payment formats, including recognized OTPs and failed or pending payment alerts, do not become suggestions. For matching messages, Runway stores the extracted amount, sender, message time, payment direction, a hashed message identifier used to prevent duplicates, and review status locally. Suggestions do not change your budget until you review and save them.

The local queue holds up to 200 entries. Entries older than 30 days are removed when the queue is next accessed. Dismissing a suggestion hides it from review but may retain its local entry until expiry or queue clearing to prevent repeat suggestions. A hashed identifier for a saved suggestion is retained in the local database to prevent the same message from being added again, even if you later delete the saved transaction.

Turn off Suggest from incoming SMS in Message suggestions to stop processing and clear the local suggestion queue. You may also revoke SMS permission in Android app settings. Revoking permission stops SMS access; turning the feature off in Runway additionally clears its queue. Neither action deletes transactions you already saved. SMS suggestions are not available on iOS.

Runway does not request precise location or GPS access, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo-library access for these features.

6. Notifications

Runway may request notification permission to provide daily reminders and purchase reminders. These notifications are scheduled and handled locally on your device. You can manage reminders in the app or disable notifications in your device settings.

7. Retention and Deletion

Your saved financial records remain in local app storage until you delete them using the app's available controls or remove app storage. To clear SMS suggestions, turn off the SMS feature as described above. To remove all local Runway data, including retained message identifiers, use your device's clear-app-storage controls or uninstall the app, where supported. Device backups and copies you exported may remain separately; manage or delete those through the relevant device, backup, or destination service. Restoring a device backup may restore saved financial records.

We do not keep your local financial records on our servers. Disabling analytics or removing local app data does not automatically erase analytics already received by Google. If you have a privacy or deletion request concerning information handled by us, contact us as described in section 10.

8. Children's Privacy

Runway does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us using the details below so we can address the concern.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Runway handles information, you can contact us through the contact information provided on the Runway Google Play Store listing.
