Runway Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Runway handles information when you use the Runway mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Runway does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers.

When you use Runway, you may choose to provide your name within the app. This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Information created or entered in Runway is stored locally on your device using local device storage.

Runway does not operate a backend server for storing your personal information, and we do not maintain a cloud database containing your app data.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Analytics and Advertising

Runway does not use third-party advertising services or analytics services to track your activity within the app.

5. Location and Device Permissions

Runway does not collect or use your precise location or GPS data.

Runway does not require access to sensitive device information such as your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or other sensitive personal data.

6. Notifications

Runway may use local notifications to provide reminders or other features within the app. These notifications are scheduled and handled locally on your device.

7. Data Deletion

Because Runway stores app data locally on your device, you can remove that data by deleting the relevant information within the app or by uninstalling the application, where applicable.

We do not retain a copy of your locally stored information on our servers.

8. Children's Privacy

Runway does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since Runway does not transmit personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a database of children's personal information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Runway handles information, you can contact us through the contact information provided on the Runway Google Play Store listing.
